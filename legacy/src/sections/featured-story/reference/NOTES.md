# Featured story (SL-21) · creative reference

**Figma:** homepage `1968:7529`, Success Stories listing `2099:519`. Screenshot: `desktop.webp` (shown with the three story cards that follow it on the homepage).

**Business point:** a real client, in their own voice, saying the partnership worked. Video makes it personal and credible.

**Why it looks this way**
- A wide card split in two: the video still on the left with the person's name in Besley over a dark bottom gradient, the story on the right.
- The glass play button is one of only two effects allowed in the system.
- On listing pages it overlaps the hero fade, so the page opens with proof.

**What can vary**
- The video (set `data-video` for an MP4 or `data-embed` for YouTube or Vimeo), still, person, logo, meta and headline.

**What must not change**
- One featured story per page.
- Headline in sentence case (not forced lowercase).
- One text link ("Read Story").
