# Services (SL-32) · creative reference

**Figma:** homepage `1968:7583` (header) and `1968:7598` (groups). Screenshot: `desktop.webp`. Tablet and mobile: `reference/pages/homepage-tablet.webp` and `homepage-mobile.webp`.

**Business point:** UXTeam sells seven services, and a visitor should find the one that matches their need in seconds. This is usually the highest weight section on a page.

**Why it looks this way**
- The only Deep Navy band on the homepage, which gives services the most visual weight.
- Services are grouped under the buyer's own question ("Not sure what's wrong?", "Ready to build?", "Want it to perform and scale?"), so visitors route themselves by need, not by our labels.
- Cards use a gradient stroke instead of a fill, so they blend into the dark band. Stacked cards share borders (−1px overlap).
- A subtle wash fades in on hover.

**What can vary**
- On other pages, the services presentation should be at least this emphasized and can be more graphical: deliverable previews, icons for what is included, tabs or expanding rows (LLM.md 12.2 and 13.10).

**What must not change**
- Service labels exactly as in LLM.md 2.3.
- Gradient strokes on dark, not white cards on dark.
- One text link per card at most.
- The band fades in and out with the spacing in LLM.md section 9 (160 top, 180 bottom).
