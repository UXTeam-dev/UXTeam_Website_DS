Sample findings

The problems an audit brings to light

Each finding comes with evidence and a clear fix. These examples are illustrative.

  • Hidden costs at checkout

    Shipping shows up only at the last step, so buyers who felt ready walk away.

  • A sign up that asks too much

    Six fields before any value is shown. Trials that never start never convert.

  • Pricing that makes people guess

    Three similar plans and no clear default, so people stall instead of choosing.

  • AI answers without a source

    Users can't tell where an answer came from, so they stop trusting it.
