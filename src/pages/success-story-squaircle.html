About UXTeam

UXTeam is a UX research and strategy driven product design partner helping digital product studios and agencies improve execution quality, delivery consistency, and conversion focused user experience outcomes.

We operate as a white label UX partner, integrating directly into existing workflows to support UX strategy, research, and design execution across complex digital projects.

About Squaircle

Squaircle is a AI Transformation Studio led by Saravanan Vijayakumar, operating across USA and India, working with clients in healthcare, fintech, and manufacturing to deliver end to end digital product solutions.

The Challenges

Scaling without losing quality or predictability

Inconsistent UX quality standards

Delivery varied project to project with no consistent baseline

Internal team bandwidth pressure

Growing project load with limited internal capacity to absorb it

No predictable delivery process

Timelines were hard to protect across multiple concurrent projects

As Squaircle scaled its project load across industries, maintaining consistent UX quality, delivery speed, and internal bandwidth became increasingly challenging.

The team needed a partner who could integrate seamlessly into their workflow, reduce execution pressure, and maintain strong UX standards across multiple concurrent client projects.

The challenge was not just about design capacity, but about creating a more predictable and reliable delivery system.

What UXTeam did

UXTeam’s approach across three delivery areas

Embedded UX Strategy Support

Integrated into Squircle’s delivery workflow to support UX strategy, research, and execution across multiple client projects.

Cross Industry UX Execution Support

Worked across healthcare, fintech, and manufacturing projects, ensuring consistent UX quality and structured delivery standards across domains.

Workflow Integration and Delivery Stabilization

Helped streamline UX execution processes, reduce dependency friction, and improve predictability across ongoing client work.
