Our story

Built by someone who came from agencies

The screen is rarely the real problem. The real problem is a decision nobody has made yet. So we start there.

We started UXTeam in January 2018. Our founder, Chintan Bhatt, came from years inside agencies and believed a specialist UXTeam could help agencies deliver better UI and UX, so they could focus on their own niche.

Today we work directly with B2B teams as well as
alongside agencies.
