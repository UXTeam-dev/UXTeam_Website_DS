## Style issues against the brand guide

- Title Case headings and buttons throughout (guide: sentence case).
- Em dashes in quotes and the founder CTA list ("–  8+ years…").
- Vocabulary on the avoid list: seamless, solutions, scalable everywhere, "high-performance".
- Mixed CTAs: "Start a Partnership", "Let’s Get Started", "Book a Call", "Book a Discovery Call", "Discuss your next event with us".
- "UI-UX", "White-Label", "AI-Assisted" hyphenation varies.
- British spellings in the privacy policy ("organisational", "unauthorised", "enquiries").
- Mobile comparison tables say "Swipe to compare ››", hiding content behind horizontal scroll.
