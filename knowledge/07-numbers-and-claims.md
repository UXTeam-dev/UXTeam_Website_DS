# Numbers and claims

Every number the live site publishes, where it appears, and whether the brand guide lets a new page use it. The brand guide's rule: **no claim goes live unless it is true on every project and we can show how we know**, and every number names what it measures, its baseline and its period.

Status key:
- **Approved:** listed as confirmed or approved in [../docs/BRAND-GUIDE.md](../docs/BRAND-GUIDE.md) section 10.
- **Published, unverified:** on the live site but not in the brand guide. Ask Chintan before reusing.
- **Do not use:** the brand guide blocks it, or the live text is a draft.

## Claims the brand guide restricts

| Claim on the live site | Brand guide position |
|---|---|
| "White-label by default", agency language on general pages | White label is secondary; keep agency language on agency pages |
| "AI-powered behavioral analysis", "AI-powered audit analysis" | Talk about AI openly, without hype. "AI-powered everything" is on the "not this" list |
| Outcome lists without numbers ("Stronger user retention", "Improved decision clarity") | A claim without a number, name or quote gets one or gets cut |
| 3Motion shown in the Product carousel | 3motionAI is **not approved**: no name, screens or figures |
| Agency partner stories (RBBI, Section, Squaircle, E2M, Trescon, DXB Live) | "Agency partner results stay private. White label work is confidential by default." These are published with permission on the live site; do not add new figures or screens without approval |
| "high-performance", "seamless", "world-class" style wording | On the avoid list |

## Safe to use without asking

- 50+ agency partners, 8+ years in business, 3 locations (India, UAE, USA), 450+ projects designed, founder's 20+ years in design.
- VORO and AgentSelect results with their approved wording.
- Published client quotes, exactly as written, with exact attribution.
- Client names and project descriptions already on the live site, used in the same context (a case study or a client list).
- Services, capabilities and process facts (these are descriptions, not claims).
