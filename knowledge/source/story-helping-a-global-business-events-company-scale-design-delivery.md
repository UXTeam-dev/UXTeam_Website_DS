About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven experience design partner that helps agencies, event organizations, and technology companies scale digital and creative delivery through embedded teams, strategic thinking, and high-quality execution.
By working as an extension of internal teams, we enable organizations to manage growing project demands while maintaining consistency, accelerating delivery, and delivering meaningful user experiences.
About Trescon
Trescon is a global business events, consulting, and media company that connects governments, enterprises, technology leaders, investors, and innovators through world-class conferences, exhibitions, and executive leadership forums.
With a strong presence across multiple international markets, Trescon creates platforms that foster knowledge sharing, business networking, and innovation across emerging industries such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, fintech, cloud computing, digital transformation, and sustainability.

Scaling design delivery across a global event portfolio
Growing project demands
Trescon’s expanding portfolio of international events and digital initiatives created increasing demand for high-quality design across multiple touchpoints.
Maintaining consistency at scale
Delivering consistent design quality across event brands, digital experiences, and marketing communications became increasingly complex.
Managing multiple timelines

Building a reliable design partnership
Trescon needed a partner who could integrate into existing workflows, understand each initiative, and deliver consistently at scale.
Trescon needed scalable design capabilities that could keep pace with its global growth without compromising quality, consistency, or delivery speed.
