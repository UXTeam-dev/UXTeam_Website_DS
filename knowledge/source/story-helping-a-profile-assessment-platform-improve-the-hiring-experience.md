About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven design partner helping SaaS companies, technology platforms, and digital businesses improve user experiences, strengthen product adoption, and accelerate business growth.
We work as an extension of internal product, marketing, and leadership teams, supporting UX strategy, UI design, and digital product execution across complex software platforms.
About HighMatch
HighMatch is a talent assessment and hiring intelligence platform that helps organizations make better hiring decisions through predictive assessments and candidate evaluation tools.
The platform enables employers to assess talent more effectively while delivering a seamless candidate experience that supports stronger engagement throughout the hiring process.
HRTech
The Challenges
Redefining the assessment experience for modern candidates
Managing growing platform complexity
As HighMatch expanded its assessment capabilities, increasing functionality introduced greater complexity across the user experience.
Streamlining the assessment journey
A functionally strong platform needed a clearer, more intuitive journey that enabled candidates to complete assessments with greater ease.
Moving beyond an interface refresh
The opportunity went beyond visual modernization. HighMatch needed to rethink the experience around candidate expectations, behavior, and engagement.
Designing for mobile-first engagement
The assessment experience needed to perform seamlessly across devices while maintaining clarity, usability, and momentum throughout the journey.
HighMatch needed to transform a complex assessment platform into a modern, intuitive experience that strengthened candidate engagement and competitive differentiation.
What UXTeam did
UXTeam’s approach across five experience areas
Assessment Portal UX Redesign
Reimagined the assessment experience to create a more intuitive, streamlined, and user-friendly journey for candidates.
Modern Mobile-First Interface Design
Designed a contemporary user experience optimized for today’s users, ensuring accessibility and usability across devices.
User Experience Optimization
Improved information architecture, interaction flows, and overall usability to reduce friction throughout the assessment process.
Collaborative Product Design Partnership

Structured Delivery & Project Management
