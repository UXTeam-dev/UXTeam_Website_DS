COMMON QUESTIONS
Frequently Asked Questions
Do you design specifically for Shopify or other platforms?
We design for multiple commerce environments including Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce, and headless commerce setups. Our role focuses on UX architecture and storefront design while your team handles platform implementation.
Can you work with our development team?
Yes. We collaborate closely with internal development teams and platform partners. Our design files and documentation support efficient implementation.
Can we engage you for only one deliverable?
Yes. Many partnerships begin with a focused scope such as product page redesign, storefront refresh, or checkout optimization.
Do you support CRO initiatives?
Yes. We regularly support agencies with UX improvements focused on conversion optimization, including checkout improvements, funnel analysis, and A/B testing design variations.
Do you provide UX audits for existing stores?
Yes. We often work with agencies reviewing existing storefronts to identify UX and conversion improvements. These audits combine interface review with behavioral signals such as navigation patterns, product discovery friction, and checkout drop-off points.
Do you follow brand guidelines provided by the client?
Yes. We regularly work with brand books and design systems provided by clients. Our role is to translate brand guidelines into scalable storefront UX while maintaining commerce functionality and conversion performance.
Will you work directly with our client?
We operate white-label by default and integrate into your agency’s communication structure. If needed, we can participate in client discussions under your direction.
How is pricing structured?
Pricing depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. Each engagement is scoped based on storefront requirements and platform realities.
