About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven product design partner helping SaaS companies and technology businesses accelerate product development without compromising user experience quality.
We operate as an embedded extension of product, engineering, and leadership teams, supporting UX strategy, UI design, design systems, and ongoing product evolution across complex digital platforms.
About Qwary
Qwary is an AI-powered experience management platform that helps organizations collect, analyze, and act on customer, employee, and market feedback.
The platform combines survey capabilities, AI-driven insights, and experience management tools to help businesses make faster and more informed decisions while improving engagement across stakeholders.

Qwary needed a reliable product design function that could integrate with its team, scale with the roadmap, and maintain a consistent standard of user experience.

Full Client Q&A
Q.
What was breaking or becoming difficult in your design delivery before you started working with UXTeam?
A.
As a product company, design is core to what we ship, but building and maintaining an in-house UX team didn’t make sense for us. We were stretched between juggling freelancers and pulling engineers into design decisions they shouldn’t have been making. Quality was inconsistent and timelines slipped often.
Q.
When evaluating partners, what made UXTeam feel like the right fit beyond just design capability?
A.
The approach felt different. There was a real effort to understand Qwary as a product, our users, and the problems we were solving, rather than just taking a brief and executing on it. Communication was clear, the team was easy to work with, and it felt like a long-term partnership from the start rather than a transactional engagement.
Q.
Where has UXTeam made the biggest difference in how your team delivers work today?
A.
They own all our UX work, so the impact is direct. Our product moves faster because design isn’t a bottleneck anymore. We maintain consistent quality across every feature we ship, and our engineering team can focus on building rather than figuring out interfaces.
Q.
How has working with UXTeam impacted your ability to scale projects or protect or improve your margins?
A.
Not having to hire and manage an in-house design team has saved us on high costs and overhead. We get senior-level design output without the fixed expense, and we can flex capacity based on what’s in the roadmap. That’s been a big factor in keeping the team lean while still moving quickly.
Q.
What’s something UXTeam does consistently that makes your life easier as a product partner?
A.
Ownership. I don’t have to chase, follow up, or repeat the context. They stay close to the product, proactively raise issues, and treat the work like it’s their own. That’s rare, and it’s the main reason this has worked so well.
Q.
If another founder asked you about UXTeam, how would you describe the partnership in one or two lines?
A.
UXTeam has been our design team from day one. If you want senior design talent that thinks like a partner and not a vendor, this is the team to work with.
