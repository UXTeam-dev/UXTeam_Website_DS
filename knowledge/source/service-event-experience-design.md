COMMON QUESTIONS
Frequently Asked Questions
Do you provide event management or production services?
No. We focus exclusively on event branding, website UI and UX, and onsite visual design systems. We do not manage logistics, vendor coordination, venue production, or platform ownership. Our role is to design the experience layer while your team leads operations and client management.
Can you work with our existing development or event platform team?
Yes. We regularly collaborate with internal development teams, external tech partners, and event platform vendors. We provide structured design files, responsive layouts, and clear handoff documentation for WordPress, Webflow, and commonly used event platforms. Your team or platform partner manages implementation while we protect design integrity.
Can we engage you for only one specific deliverable?
Yes. Many partnerships begin with a clearly defined requirement such as an event website redesign, branding refresh, sponsor kit, or onsite visual system. Starting with a focused scope allows both teams to establish collaboration rhythm before expanding further.
How do you handle tight event timelines?
Event work is milestone driven. We align our delivery schedule with your production calendar from the outset and structure design phases around launch dates, speaker announcements, sponsor approvals, and marketing rollouts. Clear revision checkpoints help avoid last minute design disruptions.
Do you use AI tools in your design process?
We use AI tools selectively to improve research efficiency and asset production speed where appropriate. However, event branding and UI-UX require structured thinking, sponsor hierarchy management, stakeholder alignment, and production discipline that extend beyond automated templates. Design direction and system thinking remain human led.
Do you support multilingual event websites?
Yes. We design multilingual ready layouts that account for content expansion, regional formatting, and audience clarity. This is especially relevant for events serving audiences across the USA, Middle East, Australia, and Singapore.
Will you work directly with our end client?
We operate white label by default and integrate into your communication structure. If required, we can participate in client discussions or presentations under your direction and positioning.
How is pricing structured?
Pricing is based on defined scope, complexity, and timeline requirements. Each engagement is scoped around the specific deliverables and production realities of the event. We provide transparent proposals aligned with project needs rather than fixed hourly packages.
