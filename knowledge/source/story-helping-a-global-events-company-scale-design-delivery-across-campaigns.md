About UXTeam
UXTeam is a design and branding partner helping agencies, event companies, and growing organizations scale creative execution without compromising quality, timelines, or operational efficiency.

About DxB Live
DxB Live is an experiential agency and an extension of DWTC (Dubai World Trade Centre), delivering industry-leading conferences, exhibitions, corporate events, and immersive brand experiences across multiple sectors.
With a geographic presence spanning the Middle East, Africa, Europe, Asia, and the USA, the organization manages complex event programs that require rapid design execution, frequent content updates, and seamless coordination across stakeholders, sponsors, and event teams.

Q.
What was breaking or becoming difficult in your design delivery before you started working with UXTeam?
A.
Prior to collaborating with UXTeam, we faced several challenges in our design delivery process. Our internal team struggled with bandwidth issues, leading to inconsistent quality in our projects. Additionally, we experienced missed timelines due to lack of clear communication and resource allocation. This often resulted in stress and frustration amongst team members, affecting overall productivity.
Q.
When evaluating partners, what made UXTeam feel like the right fit beyond just design capability?
A.
When evaluating partners, UXTeam felt like the right fit due to their collaborative approach and open communication. They demonstrated a clear understanding of our needs and were not just about design capability; their reliability and commitment to integrating seamlessly with our team stood out. This made us confident that they would not only understand our vision but also help us execute it effectively.
Q.
Where has UXTeam made the biggest difference in how your team delivers work today?
A.
UXTeam has made a significant difference in our work delivery. Their extensive experience in handling several web projects has streamlined our processes, improving our speed and predictability. They have also enabled our decision-making process by navigating new challenges such as integrating bots, managing a multilingual site, and ensuring 24/7 team availability. We now deliver projects with greater quality and confidence, knowing we have a dependable partner backing our efforts, which has greatly enhanced our overall client satisfaction.
Q.
How has working with UXTeam impacted your ability to scale projects or protect or improve your margins?
A.
Collaborating with UXTeam has positively impacted our ability to scale projects. Their support in ramping up the team as needed, especially during periods of significant workload such as pre-onsite conference preparation, where content and information changes were frequent, has been invaluable. We can now take on more work without the need to hire additional staff, leading to better utilization of our resources and ensuring a smoother project flow, which consequently helps protect our margins.
Q.
What’s something UXTeam does consistently that makes your life easier as a partner?

One of the standout qualities of UXTeam is their consistent responsiveness. They take ownership of tasks, ensuring clarity in day-to-day collaboration. This ease of partnership allows us to focus on strategy while they manage the intricacies of design, making our lives significantly simpler.
