About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven experience design partner that helps technology companies, digital businesses, and growth-stage organizations create experiences that align user behavior with business outcomes.
We work as an extension of internal teams, combining UX strategy, behavioral thinking, and scalable execution to create experiences that improve adoption, engagement, and retention.
About My Traffic Tickets
My Traffic Tickets (MTT) is a legal services platform that connects drivers with experienced traffic attorneys, helping them navigate and resolve traffic violations more efficiently. The platform simplifies what is often a complex and stressful process by providing users with access to legal expertise through a structured digital experience.

The challenges
Improving onboarding without losing users along the way
Complex onboarding journey
Users needed to understand the service and complete multiple steps with minimal confusion
Friction in the user flow
Unclear or unnecessary steps could lead to hesitation and drop-offs during onboarding
Limited user confidence
The experience needed to make users feel confident while providing the required information
Balancing UX & business goals
MTT needed to improve usability while supporting engagement, retention, and business growth
MTT needed a more intuitive onboarding experience that made it easier for users to move forward with confidence.
What UXTeam did
UXTeam’s approach across five experience areas
User Onboarding Analysis
Evaluated the existing onboarding journey to identify friction points, usability challenges, and opportunities for improvement.
Customer Journey Optimization
Mapped user behavior and refined critical flows to create a more intuitive and guided experience.
UX Strategy & Information Architecture
Improved information structure and content hierarchy to reduce confusion and help users complete tasks efficiently.
Product Experience Design
Designed interfaces that balanced usability, clarity, and business requirements while building user confidence throughout the journey.
Embedded Design Collaboration
Worked closely with the MTT team as an extension of their organization, enabling faster execution and continuous improvement.
Outcome: Improved Onboarding and Stronger User Retention
