About UXTeam
UXTeam is a design partner that helps agencies, digital transformation companies, and growing organizations scale creative delivery without the complexity of building large internal teams.
We work as an extension of existing teams, providing specialized design talent that integrates seamlessly into established workflows, enabling businesses to increase capacity while maintaining consistency and quality.
About Section
Section is a Singapore-based digital transformation company that brings together strategy, design, and technology to help organizations create future-ready digital experiences. What UXTeam did
UXTeam’s approach across five experience areas
