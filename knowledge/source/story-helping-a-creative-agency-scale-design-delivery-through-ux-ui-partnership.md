About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven experience design partner that helps agencies extend their design capabilities through embedded teams, strategic thinking, and high-quality execution.
By working as an extension of internal teams, we enable agencies to scale client delivery, maintain consistency, and confidently manage growing project demands without increasing permanent headcount.
About RBBI
RBBI is a UX, Usability, and Service Design consulting firm that helps organizations create better digital products, services, and customer experiences. Through research, usability engineering, service design, and human-centered design practices, RBBI partners with enterprises to solve complex business and customer experience challenges.
