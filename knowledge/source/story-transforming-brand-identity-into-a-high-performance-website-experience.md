The Challenges
Turning a strong brand identity into a high-performing digital experience
Brand and website disconnect
E2M had established a strong visual identity through its rebranding, but the website did not fully translate that identity into a cohesive user experience.
A visually strong, UX-light experience
The existing website focused heavily on visual presentation without enough attention to user journeys, usability, and how users consumed the information.
Balancing brand & conversion
The challenge was to preserve the new brand identity while creating clearer journeys that supported engagement and business objectives.
Designing for user expectations
The website needed to communicate E2M’s value more effectively while making it easier for users to navigate, understand, and take action.
E2M needed to turn its refreshed brand into a strategic digital experience that balanced visual impact, usability, and business performance.
What UXTeam did
UXTeam’s approach across six delivery areas
Website UX Strategy
Evaluated the existing website experience and identified opportunities to improve usability, navigation, information hierarchy, and user engagement.
User-Centered Interface Design
Created a modern website experience that balanced E2M’s new brand identity with user needs and business objectives
Brand-to-Digital Experience Translation
Transformed the newly developed brand platform into a cohesive website experience that accurately reflected E2M’s positioning as a premium white-label growth partner.
Website Journey Optimization
Improved page structures, content presentation, and interaction flows to create a clearer and more intuitive user journey.
Presentation & Visual Communication Design
Supported the creation of presentation assets aligned with E2M’s brand identity and business objectives, ensuring consistency across customer-facing touchpoints.
Collaborative Design Execution
Worked closely with stakeholders throughout the engagement, ensuring alignment between strategic goals, user expectations, and final execution.
A Website Experience That Better Reflected the Brand Vision
The partnership helped E2M bridge the gap between branding and user experience, resulting in a website that better represented the company’s expertise, positioning, and long-term vision.
