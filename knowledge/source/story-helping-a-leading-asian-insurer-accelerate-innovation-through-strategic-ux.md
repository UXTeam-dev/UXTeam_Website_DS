About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX, Usability, and Service Design consulting firm that helps enterprises, technology companies, and innovation teams transform complex business ideas into meaningful digital experiences.
Through UX research, rapid prototyping, experience strategy, and human-centered design, we enable organizations to validate ideas faster, align stakeholders more effectively, and accelerate digital innovation with confidence.
About FWD Group
FWD Group is one of Asia’s leading life and health insurance companies, serving millions of customers across multiple markets throughout the region.
Established with a vision to change the way people feel about insurance, FWD combines digital innovation, customer-centric thinking, and technology-driven experiences to simplify insurance and make financial protection more accessible. The company continues to invest in innovation initiatives that improve customer experiences, modernize insurance delivery, and create new opportunities across its diverse portfolio of markets.

Full Client Q&A
Q. What was becoming difficult before working with UXTeam?
As our innovation portfolio expanded across multiple markets, we needed a partner who could quickly transform early-stage ideas into tangible digital experiences. Managing numerous innovation initiatives simultaneously while maintaining consistency and speed required additional UX expertise that could integrate seamlessly with our teams.
Q. What made UXTeam feel like the right fit beyond design capability?
UXTeam demonstrated the ability to understand both the business context and customer experience challenges behind each initiative. Their collaborative approach, responsiveness, and ability to work as an extension of our innovation teams made the partnership highly effective.
Q. Where has UXTeam made the biggest difference?
Their biggest contribution has been enabling us to rapidly visualize and communicate innovation concepts. The quality of the prototypes helped improve stakeholder discussions, accelerate concept validation, and create greater confidence during decision-making.
Q. How has working with UXTeam impacted your ability to scale innovation?
Working with UXTeam allowed us to support multiple innovation initiatives across different markets simultaneously without building additional internal design capacity. Their ability to rapidly deliver high-quality prototypes enabled our teams to maintain momentum across a broad innovation portfolio.
Q. What’s something UXTeam consistently does that makes collaboration easier?
Ownership, responsiveness, and speed. The team adapts quickly to changing requirements, communicates proactively, and consistently delivers quality work within demanding timelines, making them a dependable extension of our innovation function.
Q. How would you describe the partnership?
UXTeam is more than a design partner—they are a trusted innovation partner who combines strategic thinking, creativity, and execution to help transform ideas into meaningful digital experiences across our regional innovation initiatives.
