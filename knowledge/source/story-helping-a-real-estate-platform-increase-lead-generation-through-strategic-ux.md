About UXTeam
UXTeam is a UX research and strategy-driven experience design partner that helps agencies, technology companies, and growing businesses create digital experiences that align user behavior with business goals.
We combine strategic UX thinking, conversion-focused design, and scalable execution to help organizations improve usability, engagement, and business performance.
About AgentSelect
AgentSelect is an Australian real estate platform that helps homeowners connect with suitable real estate agents when selling their property. Built around a customer-centric and data-driven approach, the platform simplifies the process of finding and selecting agents while helping users save time and money during one of life’s most important transactions.

The Challenges
Improving conversion without simply increasing traffic
Growing traffic, low conversions
AgentSelect was driving increasing traffic through Google Ads and Facebook, but website conversions weren’t keeping pace.
Friction in the user journey
Users were dropping off at key points, making it difficult to understand where the experience was losing potential leads.
Limited impact of internal optimizations
The team had tried incremental interface changes, but results remained inconsistent and difficult to measure.
Balancing UX & business goals
AgentSelect needed to improve usability while creating a clearer path from website visit to qualified lead.
AgentSelect needed a more strategic UX approach to identify user friction, improve the journey, and turn more website traffic into qualified leads.
What UXTeam did
UXTeam’s approach across five experience areas
UX Discovery & Conversion Analysis
Reviewed customer journeys, marketing funnels, and conversion paths to identify friction and opportunities to improve conversion.
Landing Page Optimization
Designed conversion-focused landing pages to improve lead generation and reduce visitor drop-off.
Website Experience Redesign
Restructured the website experience to better align with user expectations and decision-making behavior.
Information Architecture & Journey Design
Simplified navigation and content hierarchy to create clearer pathways toward key actions.
Ongoing Design Partnership
Continued supporting AgentSelect with landing pages, marketing assets, and experience improvements as the business evolved.
Outcome: Improved Conversion Performance Through Strategic UX
The partnership enabled AgentSelect to move beyond incremental design changes and adopt a more structured, user-centered approach to digital experiences. The first optimized landing pages generated measurable business results within weeks of launch and laid the foundation for a longer-term design partnership.
