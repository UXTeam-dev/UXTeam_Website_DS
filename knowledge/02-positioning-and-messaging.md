# Positioning and messaging on the live site

What the current site claims UXTeam is, how it says it, and how that compares with the new brand guide.

## The live positioning in one paragraph

UXTeam presents itself as a **white-label design partner for agencies**: a structured extension of an agency's team that supplies both UX thinking and dependable execution, works invisibly under the agency's brand, and lets agencies scale delivery without hiring. Each capability page repeats this as a "White-label AI Design Partner" for a type of agency (event, eCommerce, marketing, tech). The homepage hero already carries the new B2B line ("We design decisions, not just screens"), so the site is midway between the old agency positioning and the new one.

## Process: how partnerships start

Used on home, Why page and (adapted) every service page.

1. **Start with Discovery or a Defined Scope.** We begin by understanding requirements, identifying gaps, and aligning UX direction.
2. **Design & Deliver.** We execute structured UX and design aligned with your workflow.
3. **Continue & Scale.** We support ongoing work with dedicated capacity and systems.

Intro line: *We follow a clear and scalable approach built around defined scope, disciplined delivery, and long-term collaboration.*

## How the live site talks about AI

Every service hero pairs AI with human judgment:

- Event: "AI-assisted thinking and human creative direction"
- eCommerce: "AI-powered behavioral analysis and human-led UX strategy"
- Product: "AI-powered audit analysis and senior UX strategy"
- Marketing: "AI-powered behavioral analysis and human-led strategy"

FAQ answer (Event): *We use AI tools selectively to improve research efficiency and asset production speed where appropriate… Design direction and system thinking remain human led.*

This matches the brand guide's line **"We use AI. We don't outsource the decision."** Keep that framing, but drop "AI-powered" (the guide lists "AI-powered everything" under "not this").

## What clients repeatedly say (themes from 16 stories)

These are the proof points clients volunteer most. They are the strongest raw material for "why us" copy.

1. **An extension of our team, not a vendor.** Said in some form in almost every story.
2. **Responsiveness and ownership.** "take ownership of their deliverables with minimal oversight", "always just a call away".
3. **Scale without hiring.** Take on more work without permanent headcount; protects margins.
4. **Predictability.** Timelines kept, quality consistent across many projects.
5. **Strategic thinking, not just visuals.** "strong research and strategic product thinking rather than just visual design execution" (Alfred Holdings), "understands the commercial aspect of business" (AgentSelect).
6. **Speed.** "ideation to activation much faster", "high-quality design in a very short amount of time".

## Live voice vs brand guide voice

| Topic | Live site | Brand guide says |
|---|---|---|
| Reader | "your agency", "agencies" | One B2B leader, "you". Agency language only on agency pages |
| Main CTA | "Start a Partnership", "Let’s Get Started", "Book a Call" | "Book a Discovery Call" (only the story pages use it today) |
| Case | Title Case headings | Sentence case for headings and buttons |
| Vocabulary | seamless, solutions, scalable, AI-powered, high-performance | Avoid seamless, solutions, leverage, cutting-edge. Prefer diagnose, decision, metric, outcome, judgment |
| Dashes | Em dashes in quotes, hyphenated compounds (UI-UX, White-Label) | No em dashes, avoid hyphens where possible |
| Claims | Outcomes listed without baselines | Every number needs what it measures, baseline and period |
| Spelling | Mostly American; "organisational", "unauthorised" in privacy policy | American spelling |
