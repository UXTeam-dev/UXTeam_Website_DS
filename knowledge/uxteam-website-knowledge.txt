5. HOW UXTEAM WORKS
======================================================================

Process (every page):
  1. Start with Discovery or a Defined Scope: understand requirements, identify gaps, align UX direction.
  2. Design & Deliver: structured UX and design aligned with your workflow.
  3. Continue & Scale: ongoing work with dedicated capacity and systems.
  "We follow a clear and scalable approach built around defined scope, disciplined delivery, and long-term collaboration."

Start small: every capability begins with one focused piece of work (one event, one storefront, one audit or discovery, one campaign or landing page). "Starting with a focused scope allows both teams to establish collaboration rhythm before expanding further."

Engagement levels (same idea on every capability page):
  - Project-based: one clearly scoped piece of work. Good for a launch, redesign, or testing a new partner.
  - Repeat collaboration: ongoing support across several projects, campaigns or editions.
  - Retainer / ongoing support: dedicated capacity for continuous pipelines, CRO, or product updates.

Pricing: based on scope, complexity and timeline (and product stage for product work). Each engagement is scoped individually with a transparent proposal, "rather than fixed hourly packages". No prices are published.

Handoff and platforms: structured design files, responsive layouts and documentation for the client's developers. Webflow and WordPress (design and development), Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce, headless commerce, iOS and Android, common event platforms.

Working with clients of agencies: white label by default; joins client calls or presentations only under the agency's direction.

Confidentiality: NDA option on the contact form. (Brand guide confirms mutual NDA, full IP ownership and data used only for the project on every engagement.)

AI stance: each capability hero pairs AI with human judgment ("AI-assisted thinking and human creative direction", "AI-powered behavioral analysis and human-led UX strategy", "AI-powered audit analysis and senior UX strategy"). FAQ: "We use AI tools selectively to improve research efficiency and asset production speed where appropriate... Design direction and system thinking remain human led." Brand guide line to use instead: "We use AI. We don't outsource the decision."

11. FAQs (combined from all four capability pages)
======================================================================

Will you work directly with our client?
  White label by default; UXTeam fits into the agency's communication structure and joins client discussions or presentations only under the agency's direction.

How is pricing structured?
  By scope, complexity and timeline (and product stage for products). Each engagement is scoped individually with a transparent proposal, not fixed hourly packages. Usually project-based, repeat collaboration or retainer.

Can we start with just one deliverable?
  Yes. Many partnerships start with one focused piece: an event website redesign, branding refresh, sponsor kit, onsite system, product page redesign, storefront refresh, checkout optimization, UX audit or landing page.

Can you work with our development team or platform?
  Yes, with internal developers, external tech partners and platform vendors. UXTeam provides structured design files, responsive layouts and handoff documentation; your team implements while UXTeam protects design integrity.

Which platforms do you design for?
  Webflow and WordPress; Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce and headless commerce; iOS and Android; common event platforms.

Do you follow the client's brand guidelines?
  Yes. UXTeam works from brand books and design systems and turns them into scalable experiences that keep consistency and conversion performance.

Do you use AI?
  Selectively, to speed up research, audits and asset production. Design direction and system thinking remain human led.

Do you provide UX audits?
  Yes, for apps, websites and stores. Audits combine interface review with behavioral signals such as navigation patterns, discovery friction and drop-off points. Many engagements begin with one.

Do you support CRO and A/B testing?
  Yes: landing page variations, checkout improvements, funnel analysis and test variations aligned with performance cycles.

Do you support pre-sales or pitch-stage work?
  Yes: clarifying requirements, shaping UX direction and contributing to proposals.

Do you design web apps and mobile apps? MVPs? AI interfaces? Design systems?
  Yes to all: SaaS platforms, enterprise dashboards, iOS and Android apps, MVPs before development, AI interfaces that balance automation, usability and clarity, and design systems as a core part of product work.

Do you run product discovery workshops?
  Yes: structuring user journeys, product architecture and feature prioritization in early product stages.

Do you design landing pages and full marketing websites?
  Yes, including information architecture, page structure and UX aligned with lead generation.

Can you support ongoing campaigns and work with our media team?
  Yes: continuous landing pages, ad creatives and iterations, aligned with campaign goals, targeting and conversion objectives.

How do you handle tight timelines and fast turnaround?
  Delivery is aligned with the client's calendar from the start (launch dates, speaker announcements, sponsor approvals, marketing rollouts), with clear revision checkpoints and workflows built for rapid iterations and multiple variations.

Do you provide event management or production?
  No. Only event branding, website UI and UX, and onsite visual design. No logistics, vendor coordination, venue production or platform ownership.

Do you support multilingual websites?
  Yes: layouts that allow for content expansion, regional formatting and clarity, for audiences across the USA, Middle East, Australia and Singapore.
