# Success stories and portfolio

Sixteen success stories are published, plus 17 portfolio projects shown in the service page carousels. Full text of each story (including the client Q&A) is in `source/story-*.md`.

**Before naming a client or using a figure on a new page,** check [07-numbers-and-claims.md](07-numbers-and-claims.md). The brand guide says agency partner results stay private and white label work is confidential by default; these stories are published, but reuse beyond them needs Chintan's approval.

## Story page template

Every detail page has: title, intro paragraph, client representative card (photo, LinkedIn, role, company, location), Table of Contents, **Client Snapshot** (Client Type, Representative, Headquarters, Geographic Reach, Industry Focus, Engagement Type, Role of UXTeam, Key Value Areas, then three highlight tiles), About UXTeam, About [Client] with industry tag, **The Challenges** (four cards plus a summary line), **What UXTeam Did** (three to six cards), **The Outcome** (numbered list), **Who This Is For**, **Full Client Q&A** (six standard questions), **Client Testimonial**, founder CTA, related stories.

The six Q&A questions (same on every story):
1. What was breaking or becoming difficult in your design delivery before you started working with UXTeam?
2. When evaluating partners, what made UXTeam feel like the right fit beyond just design capability?
3. Where has UXTeam made the biggest difference in how your team delivers work today?
4. How has working with UXTeam impacted your ability to scale projects or protect or improve your margins?
5. What’s something UXTeam does consistently that makes your life easier as an agency partner?
6. If another agency asked you about UXTeam, how would you describe the partnership in one or two lines?

## Story summaries

### 1. RBBI: Helping a Creative Agency Scale Design Delivery Through UX/UI Partnership
- **Client:** RBBI, a UX, Usability and Service Design consulting firm (snapshot: "Brand Strategy & Creative Agency"), India HQ, global clientele.
- **Engagement:** Embedded Agency Partnership; dedicated UX/UI support for client deliverables.
- **Challenge:** Growing pipeline, peaks of demand, limited bandwidth, needed to keep quality and work inside RBBI's workflows.
- **What UXTeam did:** Embedded UX/UI support; flexible resource scaling at peaks; collaborative delivery; consistent execution across clients; seamless integration.
- **Outcomes:** More capacity at peaks; consistency across engagements; quality kept on tight timelines; took on more work without permanent headcount; fewer bottlenecks; long-term partnership.

### 2. E2M Solutions: Transforming Brand Identity into a High-Performance Website Experience
- **Client:** E2M, a white-label growth partner for digital agencies (web, marketing, SEO, PPC, AI) with over a decade serving agencies in the USA, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia.
- **Engagement:** White-Label Partnership; website UX/UI and presentation design after a rebrand.
- **Challenge:** Strong new brand, but the website was "visually strong, UX-light": poor journeys, usability and information consumption.
- **What UXTeam did (six areas):** Website UX strategy; user-centered interface design; brand-to-digital translation; journey optimization; presentation design; collaborative execution.
- **Outcomes:** Brand translated into a user-focused site; better usability and navigation; professional, structured delivery; "reliable and cost-effective design partnership".

### 3. Trescon: Helping a Global Business Events Company Scale Design Delivery
- **Client:** Global business events, consulting and media company, Dubai HQ; reach across Middle East, Europe, Africa, Asia, North America; topics like AI, cybersecurity, fintech, cloud.
- **Engagement:** Long-term embedded design partnership: event websites, marketing collateral, event branding, UI/UX, creative execution.
- **Challenge:** Growing event portfolio, consistency at scale, many timelines.
- **What UXTeam did:** Event website design; branding and visual identity; marketing collateral (brochures, social, campaigns, presentations); embedded support; scalable creative delivery.
- **Outcomes:** More creative capacity across global events; consistent branding; faster execution; scaled without permanent headcount.

### 4. Alfred Holdings: Driving UX Strategy & Conversion-Focused Design Across Its Platforms
- **Client:** Dubai digital holding company running InsuranceMarket.ae (insurance comparison and purchase) and InvestmentMarket.ae (investment discovery).
- **Engagement:** UX audit, journey optimization, UX strategy and product design across both platforms; "Multi-Platform UX Partnership".
- **Challenge:** Two platforms with different goals but shared problems: decision complexity, friction, inconsistent conversion paths; needed ecosystem alignment without internal complexity.
- **What UXTeam did (three areas):** Separate UX audits and cross-platform journey analysis; UX-strategy-led redesign of key flows; experience optimization for decision clarity and lower cognitive load.
- **Outcomes:** Better decision clarity on both platforms; consistent UX structure; less friction in complex flows; stronger conversion-focused journeys; scalable UX foundation.

### 5. DXB Live: Helping a Global Events Company Scale Design Delivery Across Campaigns
- **Client:** The experiential agency of Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC); conferences, exhibitions, corporate events across the Middle East, Africa, Europe, Asia, USA.
- **Engagement:** Event website design (including multilingual and third-party integrations), marketing collateral, branding, ongoing creative support.
- **Challenge:** Growing conference schedule, limited bandwidth, frequent last-minute content changes before events, quality at scale.
- **What UXTeam did:** Event websites and digital experiences; branding and collateral; capacity during peak cycles; rapid pre-event updates (agendas, speakers, sites); embedded workflow collaboration.
- **Outcomes (eight):** Consistent delivery across events; faster response in pre-event crunch; scaled without hiring; multilingual support; improved client satisfaction; predictability. Client mentions bots integration, multilingual site and "24/7 team availability".

### 6. FWD Group: Helping a Leading Asian Insurer Accelerate Innovation Through Strategic UX
- **Client:** One of Asia's leading life and health insurers, Hong Kong HQ; markets include Hong Kong, Macau, Thailand, Cambodia, Vietnam, Japan, Philippines, Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia.
- **Engagement:** Enterprise Innovation & UX Prototyping Partnership. **19 high-fidelity prototypes across 11 Asian markets.**
- **Challenge:** Many market contexts, rapid concept validation, parallel initiatives, speed with consistency for leadership reviews.
- **What UXTeam did:** Enterprise UX prototyping; cross-market support; experience strategy and concept visualization; executive investment dashboard and presentations; embedded with innovation teams.
- **Projects supported:** Financially Broke (Cambodia, expense tracker); Pocket Pal and FWD Revive (Group Office); Innovative Distribution Model, End-to-End Tracking System, AI for Group Underwriting (Indonesia); My Number Service Platform, FWD Life Coach (Japan); Affiliate+, FWD WithU (Malaysia); Magsasa Can, Plant a Seed (Philippines); Next Gen-Er (AI hologram assistant), #IYKYK (Singapore); Legacy Log (Thailand); Independent Agency Model, Next Alpha (Vietnam).
- **Outcomes:** Faster visualization of concepts; faster stakeholder alignment; consistency across initiatives; supported executive investment decisions.

### 7. Squaircle: Premium UX Strategy Partnership for a Digital Product Studio
- **Client:** AI transformation studio (USA and India) serving healthcare, fintech and manufacturing clients.
- **Engagement:** White-label, embedded UX research and strategy partner across client projects.
- **Highlight numbers:** **Turnaround 30% faster; review rounds 3 → 1.**
- **Challenge:** Inconsistent UX quality, bandwidth pressure, no predictable delivery process.
- **What UXTeam did (three areas):** Embedded UX strategy; cross-industry execution; workflow integration and delivery stabilization.
- **Outcomes:** Predictable delivery; consistent UX quality across industries; less coordination load; confidence to scale.
- **Note:** This is the story the repo's coded "Success Story detail" page is built from.

### 8. Section: Helping a Digital Transformation Company Scale Design Delivery
- **Client:** Singapore digital transformation company (Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam).
- **Engagement:** Dedicated UI design resource embedded in Section's team. "0 Additional Headcount."
- **What UXTeam did:** Dedicated UI designer; design library creation; designer presented directly to clients; weekly check-ins; flexible capacity.
- **Outcomes:** More capacity without hiring; better quality and consistency; faster onboarding; confidence to pursue new business. Client: "Chintan and his leadership team are highly responsive and always just a call away."

### 9. Enoviq: Helping an InsurTech Company Simplify Complex Ideas With Presentation Design
- **Client:** InsurTech company modernizing insurance operations for insurers and enterprise partners; global reach.
- **Engagement:** Strategic presentation design: one corporate company presentation.
- **What UXTeam did:** Presentation strategy and information design; visual storytelling; executive presentation design; iterative collaboration; flexible support.
- **Outcomes:** Polished executive presentation; complex concepts simplified; no extra in-house design hires needed.

### 10. AgentSelect: Helping a Real Estate Platform Increase Lead Generation through Strategic UX
- **Client:** Australian platform matching homeowners with real estate agents; Melbourne.
- **Engagement:** Strategic UX partnership: research, conversion-focused design, IA, landing pages, website experience ("Research → UX → UI").
- **Challenge:** Growing paid traffic from Google Ads and Facebook but conversions not keeping pace; earlier incremental UI tweaks gave inconsistent results.
- **What UXTeam did:** UX discovery and conversion analysis; landing page optimization; website restructure; IA and journey design; ongoing partnership.
- **Outcomes:** **20–30% increase in lead generation and conversion performance after launch**, results "within weeks of launch"; clearer journeys; long-term partnership.

### 11. Elision Technologies: Helping Modernize a Complex Contact Center Solution Through UX Design
- **Client:** Ahmedabad enterprise communication company; flagship DialShree contact center (inbound, outbound, blended campaigns; admin, supervisor and agent apps; omnichannel, CRM integrations, AI voice).
- **Engagement:** Product UX modernization; multi-module UX; modern Figma design system.
- **What UXTeam did:** Product and workflow discovery; UX modernization of flows; IA; consistent UI; Figma design system and close collaboration.
- **Outcomes (nine):** Modernized UX/UI; consistency across modules; Figma foundation for development; less ambiguity and rework; confidence presenting to customers; capacity without big hiring.

### 12. BlueGenAI: Strategic UX Partnership for an AI Startup Modernizing Public Services
- **Client:** AI platform helping government agencies and systems integrators modernize software development (GovTech).
- **Engagement:** Website design and digital experience strategy at an early growth stage.
- **Outcomes:** Professional website launched in an important growth phase; stronger credibility; leadership stayed focused on product.

### 13. My Traffic Tickets (MTT): UX Partnership with a Legal Services Platform to Improve Onboarding and Retention
- **Client:** US platform connecting drivers with traffic attorneys.
- **Engagement:** Product UX partnership: research, journey design, product UX, IA, UI.
- **Challenge:** Complex multi-step onboarding causing hesitation and drop-off; low user confidence.
- **Outcomes:** Better onboarding; less friction; stronger retention and engagement (no figures).

### 14. HighMatch: Helping a Profile Assessment Platform Improve the Hiring Experience
- **Client:** US talent assessment and hiring intelligence platform.
- **Engagement:** Assessment portal UX and UI redesign, mobile first, candidate experience.
- **Outcomes:** Modern, intuitive assessment; "increased assessment redemption and completion rates" (no figure); differentiation against competitors with older experiences; supported sales conversations and helped close new business.

### 15. Voro: Scalable Design Partnership for a Fast-Growing Real Estate Technology Company
- **Client:** New York cloud-based real estate brokerage empowering independent agents.
- **Engagement:** Ongoing embedded design across business initiatives (branding, UI/UX, website, marketing assets).
- **Outcomes:** Faster execution; consistency; less bottleneck at peaks; more projects without aggressive hiring.
- **Approved numbers (brand guide, not on the live story):** agent sign-ups 4.0% to 5.4%, onboarding 21% to 30.7%, from a 4-week homepage A/B test with about 600K visitors per variant.

### 16. Qwary: Product Design Partnership for Fast-Growing Experience Management Platform
- **Client:** US AI-powered experience management platform (surveys, AI insights, CX/EX).
- **Engagement:** Dedicated, embedded product design partner across platform and marketing website.
- **Challenge:** Fragmented freelancers, engineers making design decisions, inconsistent delivery, needed to keep pace with the roadmap.
- **Outcomes (eight):** Removed design bottlenecks; consistency; engineers back on engineering; faster features; no in-house UX team needed. Quote: "UXTeam has been our design team from day one."
