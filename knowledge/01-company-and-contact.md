## After the form: Thank You page

> **Thank You for Reaching Out**
> We’ve received your submission and our team is reviewing the details. You can expect to hear from us within 24 hours.

**How We’ll Move Forward Together**
1. **Review & Qualification.** We’ll align on your project goals, scope, and timeline to ensure we’re fully synchronized.
2. **Strategy Alignment Call.** If we’re aligned, we’ll schedule a call to discuss priorities and outline the execution framework.
3. **Structured Proposal.** Based on our discussion, we’ll provide a clear proposal outlining the next steps, timeline, and deliverables.

Commitments stated: reply **within 24 hours** of a form submission; privacy enquiries answered **within 5 business days**.
