# Services (the four capability pages)

The live site sells four capabilities. All four pages share one template, so they are documented side by side, then one by one. Exact wording is in `source/service-*.md`.

The brand guide replaces these four with **seven services** (UX audit, UX strategy workshop, Product and app design, Web and marketing design, Conversion optimization, Design systems, AI design for B2B products) and three ways to work (Audit, Project, Partnership). Use the detail below as raw material for those pages. Mapping:

| Live capability | Feeds these brand guide services |
|---|---|
| Product & App Experience Design | Product and app design, UX audit, Design systems, AI design for B2B products |
| Marketing Experience Design | Web and marketing design, Conversion optimization |
| eCommerce Experience Design | Conversion optimization, Web and marketing design (eCommerce is a D2C, supporting audience) |
| Event & Experience Design | Web and marketing design; event platforms are a named industry |

## Shared page template

1. Hero: "White-label AI Design Partner for X", one line, "Start a Partnership".
2. **MILESTONES WE ACHIEVED / Success in Numbers:** four stats.
3. **DELIVERED AT SCALE / Selected X Work:** carousel of client projects with location/sector, a one-line description and "Our Deliverables" chips.
4. Inline CTA: "Discuss your next … with us".
5. **WHAT WE EXECUTE / Our X Design Capabilities:** three or four capability cards, each with a line and a bullet list.
6. **WHAT SETS US APART / Why X Choose UXTeam:** five-row "UXTeam Approach vs Typical Approach" table (swipe on mobile).
7. **BUILT FOR SCALE / How We Support X:** six or seven support points.
8. Inline CTA: "Looking for structured white-label X design support?"
9. **COLLABORATION FRAMEWORK:** three steps (start small, design and deliver, continue and scale).
10. **ENGAGEMENT MODELS / How to Engage Us:** three models, each with "Ideal for".
11. **COMMON QUESTIONS / Frequently Asked Questions** (see [06-faqs.md](06-faqs.md)).
12. **WORK WITH US / Start a Partnership for X:** the contact form.

## 1. Event & Experience Design

**Capabilities**

- **Event Branding.** Scalable visual identities built for conferences, summits, and multi-edition programs. Event logo and visual identity; brand guidelines and asset systems; speaker and sponsor templates; sponsor branding placements; event theme and campaign visuals; multi-edition brand continuity.
- **Event Websites & UX.** Structured and conversion-focused design for event websites and registrations. Event website UI and UX design; registration and ticket page design; agenda and speaker layouts; sponsor showcase pages; session detail and track pages; multilingual-ready layouts; WordPress and Webflow development; platform-compatible design handoff.
- **Marketing & Onsite Design.** Consistent visual systems across promotion and venue environments. Social media creatives; email and launch campaigns; digital ads and banners; stage backdrops and screen graphics; signage and wayfinding; badge and print design; exhibition booth graphics; sponsor visibility systems.

**UXTeam vs typical** (intro: *Event agencies operate under intense timelines and stakeholder pressure.*)

| | UXTeam | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| Event Expertise | Dedicated event branding and experience design | General design studios handling event work occasionally |
| Scalable Capacity | Scalable white-label design support | In-house teams stretched during peak cycles |
| Structured Execution | Structured event brand and UX systems | Freelancers delivering fragmented assets |
| AI-Assisted Efficiency | AI-assisted workflows with strategic design | Template-driven AI design outputs |
| Design-Led Integration | Design-led event experiences across platforms | Platform-driven layouts |

**How we support event agencies:** Timeline Alignment (design delivery around your event calendar, milestones and launch deadlines); Sponsor & Stakeholder Sensitivity (sponsor hierarchies, approvals, multi-stakeholder inputs); Scalable Design Systems (across editions, formats and assets); High-Volume Asset Management (structured revision control and version discipline); Multilingual & Regional Readiness (USA and Middle East audiences); White-Label Collaboration.

**Framework:** Start With One Event (a clearly defined scope for a single event) → Design & Deliver (branding and UI-UX across digital and onsite touchpoints with structured revision control and deadline discipline) → Continue & Scale (additional events, seasonal cycles, ongoing partnerships).

**Engagement models:** Project-Based Engagement (a specific event; ideal for launching a new event, refreshing an edition, beginning a collaboration) · Repeat Event Collaboration (recurring conferences or multiple editions a year) · Ongoing Event Design Support (priority support for continuous calendars or multi-client portfolios; high-volume pipelines).

**What UXTeam does not do:** event management, logistics, vendor coordination, venue production or platform ownership.

## 2. eCommerce Experience Design

**Capabilities**

- **Storefront UX Design.** Conversion-focused storefront architecture aligned with brand systems. Homepage and campaign landing pages; collection and category pages; product detail page UX; search and filtering systems; navigation architecture; mobile commerce design; translating brand guidelines into scalable storefront UI systems.
- **Conversion & Checkout Optimization.** UX improvements informed by behavioral data and conversion patterns. Cart and checkout flow UX; upsell and cross-sell modules; trust and purchase confidence design; A/B testing design variations; conversion funnel improvements; store UX audits based on behavioral and performance data; mobile checkout optimization.
- **Scalable Commerce Design Systems.** Storefront design systems; product template structures; merchandising modules; campaign landing frameworks; platform-ready handoff (Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce); ongoing storefront maintenance and campaign support.

**UXTeam vs typical** (intro: *eCommerce development teams often manage platform builds, integrations, and performance optimization. UXTeam supports them with structured design expertise focused on conversion and scalable storefront systems.*)

| | UXTeam | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion Optimization | Conversion-focused storefront UX and purchase flows | Standard storefront layouts focused on structure |
| Product Discovery UX | Structured navigation, filtering, and browsing systems | Conventional category and browsing structures |
| Brand Consistency | Storefront UX aligned with brand systems | Brand guidelines applied across individual pages |
| AI-Assisted Workflows | AI-assisted workflows with CRO insights | AI-generated layouts for faster asset creation |
| Scalable Storefront Systems | Reusable storefront frameworks for campaigns and growth | Page-by-page design based on project needs |

**Support points:** Conversion Architecture; Catalog Complexity (large catalogs, variants, merchandising logic); Brand System Translation; Platform & Campaign Flexibility; Pre-Sales Collaboration; White-Label Collaboration.

**Framework:** Start With One Storefront (store launch, redesign or CRO initiative) → Design & Optimize → Continue & Scale (ongoing CRO, campaign launches, storefront evolution).

**Engagement models:** Project-Based (launch, redesign or CRO initiative) · Repeat Commerce Collaboration (multiple storefronts or campaigns) · eCommerce Design Retainer (dedicated capacity for consistent UX and CRO support).

## 3. Product & App Experience Design

**Capabilities**

- **UX Audit & Product Experience Evaluation.** UX and interface evaluation; behavioral flow analysis; conversion friction identification; navigation and interaction review; product usability insights; prioritized UX improvement roadmap.
- **Product Discovery & UX Architecture.** Product discovery workshops; user journey mapping; information architecture; feature prioritization; MVP planning and validation; product workflow structuring.
- **Product Interface & Interaction Design.** SaaS dashboards; web application interfaces; mobile app UX (iOS and Android); AI product interfaces; interaction design for complex workflows; responsive product UI.
- **Scalable Product Design Systems.** Component libraries; design tokens and UI systems; developer-friendly UI kits; consistent interaction patterns; feature scalability frameworks; design system documentation.

**UXTeam vs typical** (intro: *Technology companies often focus heavily on engineering and platform architecture. UXTeam complements them with structured UX expertise, scalable design systems, and AI-assisted efficiency.*)

| | UXTeam | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| Product UX Expertise | Structured product UX and interaction design | UI-focused design without deeper product thinking |
| UX Audit Insights | UX audits combining usability and conversion insights | Surface-level interface reviews |
| Scalable Design Systems | Design systems built for long-term product growth | Page-by-page interface design |

**Framework:** Start With an Audit or Discovery → Design & Optimize (interfaces, interaction systems, prototypes for development readiness) → Continue & Scale (feature expansion, design system growth).

**Engagement models:** Project-Based (UX audit, product launch or feature design) · Product Design Collaboration (across development cycles; multiple features or platforms) · Ongoing Product Design Support (dedicated capacity; ongoing updates, feature tweaks, UX improvements).

## Cross-service facts worth reusing

- **Every engagement can start small:** one event, one storefront, one audit, one landing page. "Starting with a focused scope allows both teams to establish collaboration rhythm before expanding further."
- **Three engagement levels everywhere:** project, repeat collaboration, retainer or ongoing support. These map to the brand guide's Project and Partnership.
- **Pricing** is always "based on scope, complexity, and timeline", proposals are transparent, "rather than fixed hourly packages". No prices are published.
- **Handoff:** structured design files, responsive layouts and documentation for the client's developers. Event and marketing pages also mention WordPress and Webflow development.
- **Regions named on service pages:** USA, Middle East, Australia, Singapore (events); North America (Supra ITS); India (Fitcart); Dubai (several).
